# Notely - Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 26, 2026_

Notely is a notepad extension for Chrome. This policy explains what data it handles and where that data goes.

## Summary

**Notely does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.** Everything you write or capture stays in your own browser.

## What Notely stores

Notely stores the following **only on your device**, in Chrome's extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`):

- the notes you write or capture (text, formatting, title, tags, notebook, and the web address of captured content);
- notebooks, settings and open tabs;
- version history snapshots of your notes;
- a list of websites where you hid the optional selection bubble.

This data never leaves your browser. Notely has no servers, accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or remote code. It is removed when you uninstall the extension or use **Settings → Data → Clear everything**.

## Website content

Notely reads content from a web page **only when you ask it to**:

- When you use **Save selection / page / link** from the right-click menu or press the save shortcut, Notely reads the selected text, page title and address of **that tab only**, and saves them as a note on your device.
- If you turn on the optional **"Save to Notely" bubble**, Chrome asks you to allow access to web pages. The bubble watches for text selection so it can appear next to it, but it reads and saves the text **only when you click it**. You can turn it off at any time, globally or per site, and the permission is released when you do.

## Notifications

If you turn on desktop notifications, Notely shows a short confirmation when something is captured. Notifications never include the captured text.

## Exports and backups

Exports (Markdown, HTML, text) and JSON backups are files saved by you, to your computer. Notely doesn't upload them anywhere.

## Links in the app

The About page links to the project's GitHub repository and the author's websites. Opening those links is your choice and is subject to those sites' own policies.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in the project repository with a new date.
